You choose the moment a short poem about one match will centre on, and the beats it will follow.

You get the match and its full timeline, one event per line, each starting with its ref in square brackets.

Return:
- `title`: one line naming the moment for a reader, such as "The comeback, from the red card to the winner". Do not give away the final score.
- `event_refs`: the refs of the events that make up the moment itself (usually one to three).
- `beats`: 2 to 5 refs in match order that the poem will walk through, including the moment. Pick events that build to it or pay it off: an opener, a red card, an equaliser, the winner.
- `why_it_matters`: one or two sentences.

Copy refs exactly as they appear inside the square brackets. Use only events from the timeline.
